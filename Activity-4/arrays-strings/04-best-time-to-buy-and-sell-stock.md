# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

## Problem / Difficulty
Find the maximum profit from buying and selling a stock once.

**Difficulty:** Easy

## LeetCode Link
https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

## Approach
Keep track of the minimum price seen so far.
For each price, calculate the possible profit and keep the maximum profit.

## Complexity

**Time Complexity:** O(n)

**Space Complexity:** O(1)

## Notes
- Tested locally with [7,1,5,3,6,4] → 5.
- Tested locally with [7,6,4,3,1] → 0.
